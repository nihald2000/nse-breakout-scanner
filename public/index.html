Live prices refresh every 10 seconds while the market is open: LTP, Chg %, Dist %, Rel vol, Range pos, Status and Ref stop update in place. The list of stocks and the Score are refreshed by a full scan every 3 minutes, and new entries are tagged NEW. Level: 20-day high (long) or low (short). Dist %: how far price is beyond it (negative = still below). Status: BREAKOUT/BREAKDOWN past the level, NEAR within 0.5% of it, FADED if it has dropped back more than 0.5% under it. Rel vol: today's volume vs the 20-day average, projected to a full day (rough during market hours). Range pos: 1.00 means price is at the day's extreme. Ref stop: today's low (long) or high (short), a reference only. Stock lists come from NSE; prices come from Yahoo Finance, polled rather than streamed, and may lag NSE. The "last tick" time shows how fresh the newest price is. This is a screening aid, not trading advice.
