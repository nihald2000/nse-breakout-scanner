Ichimoku Cloud Scanner

NSE stocks that have just closed above (or below) the Ichimoku cloud, with Tenkan/Kijun, Chikou and future-cloud confirmation.

Note: the 15 min and 1 hour scans here are informational only. The Call is a checklist on the cloud, not a forecast; the intraday index rules (ADX trend filter, no entries before 10:00) live on the Index Options page.

What this finds: stocks whose latest close is beyond the Ichimoku cloud (settings 9 / 26 / 52) after being at or inside it on the previous bar. Cloud edge is the top of the cloud for longs and the bottom for shorts, as drawn at the latest bar (hover for the full range). Dist % is how far price is beyond that edge; negative means still short of it.

Status: BREAKOUT / BREAKDOWN = the latest bar made the break. RECENT = broke 1 to 5 bars ago and is still beyond the cloud. NEAR = close to the edge but not through it. FADED = has dropped back since the scan.

Confirms (lit = agrees with the trade direction): TK Tenkan beyond Kijun, KJ price beyond Kijun, CH Chikou clear (today's close is beyond the candles and the cloud of 26 bars ago), FC the cloud 26 bars ahead is coloured the same way. Score weighs freshness and these confirmations, plus relative volume on Daily. Rel vol is Daily only; 1 hour and 15 min bars carry no volume reading here. Ref stop: for a fresh break, the Kijun or the far side of the cloud, whichever is closer; for NEAR, the last 5 bars' swing low or high. A reference only. Hover a Cloud edge value to see the whole cloud, its width and where the break came from.

Call is a checklist, not a forecast. It shows BUY (long) or SELL (short sale) only when all five checks pass, otherwise WAIT: (1) price is 0.3% to 2.5% beyond the cloud, (2) the break is 3 bars old or less, (3) at least 3 of the 4 confirms are lit, (4) Daily only: relative volume is 1.2x or more, (5) the reference stop is within 5% of price. The % is how many checks pass, and hovering the badge lists them. For BUY / SELL the line underneath gives the plan as % moves from the current price: SL at the reference stop, T1 at 2x and T2 at 3x that risk. For WAIT it says what is missing, or the close you are waiting for. The Call updates with the live price, so it can flip. It is built for the Daily timeframe; on 1 hour and 15 min the same rules apply to those bars. A SELL held overnight needs an F&O stock, since cash-segment shorts must be closed the same day.

The latest bar is still forming while the market is open, so a break can reverse before it closes. On Daily the latest bar is today's. Live prices refresh every 10 seconds and update LTP, Chg %, Dist %, Rel vol and Status against the cloud as drawn at the last scan. Confirmations and score refresh with a full scan every 3 minutes, and new entries are tagged NEW.

Stock lists come from NSE; prices come from Yahoo Finance, polled rather than streamed, and may lag NSE. Only stocks priced at ₹50 or more with average daily turnover of ₹5 crore or more are scanned. This is a screening aid, not trading advice.
