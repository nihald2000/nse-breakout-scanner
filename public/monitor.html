Waiting for the Stocks in Play scan…

The first five-minute candle must trade at least its normal volume (1.0× the same candle's 14-session average). Its colour sets the side: a stop order at its high (CE) or low (PE), stop loss 10% of the 14-day ATR, no target, flat at 15:15 IST. PLAN means the order has not been touched yet; OPEN means a closed candle has touched it. From a US-equity study (Zarattini, Barbon & Aziz, 2024); it has not been tested on NSE, and the tight stop means costs can eat a large part of an R, shown on each card.
