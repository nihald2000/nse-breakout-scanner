Sector Scope

Which F&O industry group has the most money behind it today, and the stocks inside it with running volume. Updates itself every minute — nothing to press; it shows where things stand and you make the trade.

F&O stocks in

Sorted by running volume (today's cumulative volume at this clock minute vs the mean of the earlier sessions in the same fetch). CE / PE is the side the checks lean — CE = the facts point up (call side), PE = down (put side), with how one-sided they are: one-sided = most checks agree and ADX and structure point the same way; building = a majority with partial support; a dash = two-way, no lean. It is a count of agreeing facts, not a probability of profit and not an order. Tenkan(9)/Kijun(26) use closed candles of the chosen timeframe; the reference stop is the Kijun of that timeframe and is a fact about the chart, not a stop for you. Delivery % is the previous completed session's from NSE's own file. F&O ban is not checked (no free source). Option bid/ask is not shown because no free live chain exists — check your broker before buying. Lines drawn on candles change with the next candle; do not treat a tag as an order.

The sector % here is the turnover-weighted average of the F&O stocks in the group — not the NSE sector index. NSE publishes its own sector levels, but its live sector page is not reachable from cloud servers, so the members' own prices are the source. "Normal" turnover is the mean of the prior 20 sessions scaled by the expected share of the day elapsed.
