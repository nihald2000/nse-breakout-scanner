Paper Trade Journal

Log the option trades you would take from the Index Options page, using the real option premiums you see in your broker. Everything stays in this browser (local storage); nothing is sent anywhere.

Paper results are the only out-of-sample evidence you will have. 20–30 sessions is the minimum; 50+ is better. Log the signals you skipped too, otherwise the record only shows the trades you liked. This is a record of your own trades, not proof of an edge.

Add an entry

Option R = (exit − entry) ÷ (entry − planned stop premium). Leave the stop blank if you have none; the % result still counts. Leave the exit blank while a trade is open. For a skipped signal only the date, index, strategy and side are needed.

Backtest vs paper

Backtest index R and option R (Black-Scholes approximation, no IV change) come from the last backtest run in this browser on the Index Options page. Paper option R comes from your entries above. They are not the same thing: the backtest is a model over about 40 sessions, and a paper log of a few trades is mostly noise. Do not read a gap as proof either way; read it as a prompt to look at your trades one by one.
