Scanned groups are the hottest F&O industry groups by absolute turnover-weighted % move (2+ stocks), both directions, plus the money ratio. CE / PE is the side the checks lean — CE = the facts point up, PE = down — strong (most checks agree and ADX + structure agree) or moderate (a majority with partial support); weak reads are counted in the status line but not listed. Rows are ordered strong before moderate, then more agreeing checks, more context signals, then higher running volume; the Show cap hides the tail of that order, not a judgement. Last ref is the last closed 15-minute candle at the time of the read. It is not an entry. Now is the last traded price, with its drift from that reference. Prior high / low are the actual previous session's high and low, drawn as reference levels; this directional score has no validated entry, stop or target. ATM option is the nearest-strike CE (for a CE read) or PE (for a PE read) from NSE's option-chain snapshot at the front expiry: the premium is the bid/ask mid, with the quoted spread, IV, delta and OI. It is market data, not a fill; the quote can move before you act. The F&O ban list is a separate NSE publication and is not checked here — check it with your broker.
