One board instead of opening every group: the stocks whose closed-candle checks lean one side, strong and moderate reads only. Each row carries the side, the reference bar close, the ±0.5% levels the replay was measured on and the Kijun line. This is a count of facts, not advice — you choose the contract, the strike and the size, and you carry the stop.

Replay on the last 20 sessions (38 stocks, closed 15-minute bars, entry at the signal bar close, underlying only, no costs): strong reads hit +0.5% before −0.5% in 63.6% of decidable cases (n=55, small), moderate in 52.3% (n=543), weak in 51.3% (n=507), horizon 90 minutes. A 1:1 payoff needs more than 50% just to break even, and option spread, premium and costs are not part of that number. Treat every row as a fact to check, not a promise.

Scanned groups are the hottest F&O industry groups by absolute turnover-weighted % move (2+ stocks), both directions, plus the money ratio. CE / PE is the side the checks lean — CE = the facts point up, PE = down — strong (most checks agree and ADX + structure agree) or moderate (a majority with partial support); weak reads are counted in the status line but not listed. Rows are ordered strong before moderate, then more agreeing checks, more context signals, then higher running volume; the Show cap hides the tail of that order, not a judgement. Ref close is the last closed 15-minute candle at the time of the read, the price the replay entered at. Now is the last traded price, with its drift from the ref close — if it has already run without you, the levels belong to the old entry, so re-anchor to your fill. Target / Stop are the tested ±0.5% barriers on the stock; on an option the premium moves roughly with delta × the stock move, but IV and time decay are not modeled here. F&O ban and option bid/ask are not checked (no free source) — check your broker before buying. Click any row for its candlestick chart and full read. Screening aid, not trading advice.
