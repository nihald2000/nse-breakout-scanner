# Side Stock Sector Status 1st-candle vol Stop order Stop loss Risk / share Costs Now Result Cautions

Every F&O stock whose first five-minute candle (09:15–09:20) traded at least its normal volume (1.0× the same candle's 14-session average) is listed, highest first; the paper trades the top 20, marked top 20, and none is hidden. The candle's colour sets the side: a stop order at its high (CE) or low (PE) with no retest, a stop loss of 10% of the 14-day ATR from the entry, no target, flat at 15:15 IST. PLAN = the order has not been touched yet; OPEN = a closed candle touched it; Result is in R (multiples of that stop distance) and is a mark-to-market figure until the trade is stopped or flat. Costs is the assumed 0.10% round trip expressed in R: with this tight a stop it can be a large fraction of an R before the trade moves. From a US-equity study (Zarattini, Barbon & Aziz, 2024, 2016–2023); not tested on NSE here, so nothing on it is a proven edge. Levels are for the underlying share, from closed Yahoo candles that can be a few minutes late; check the first-candle volume, the option contract and the F&O ban list yourself. Scanned groups are the hottest F&O industry groups by absolute turnover-weighted % move (2+ stocks), both directions, plus the money ratio. CE / PE is the side the checks lean — CE = the facts point up, PE = down — strong (most checks agree and ADX + structure agree), moderate (a majority with partial support) or weak (a thin majority, little confirmation). All of them are listed; use Strong only to cut the list. Rows are ordered strong, moderate, weak, then more agreeing checks, more context signals, then higher running volume. Option quotes are fetched for the best-ranked rows only; a row without one says so. Last ref is the last closed 15-minute candle at the time of the read. It is not an entry. Now is the last traded price, with its drift from that reference. Prior high / low are the actual previous session's high and low, drawn as reference levels; this directional score has no validated entry, stop or target. ATM option is the nearest-strike CE (for a CE read) or PE (for a PE read) from NSE's option-chain snapshot at the front expiry: the premium is the bid/ask mid, with the quoted spread, IV, delta and OI. It is market data, not a fill; the quote can move before you act. The F&O ban list is a separate NSE publication and is not checked here — check it with your broker.
